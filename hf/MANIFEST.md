# Released models: candidate list

**Status: proposal; nothing has been uploaded.** The repository names follow the proposal in [README.md](README.md)
and may change. Each card in `cards/` is generated by `make_cards.py` from the paper's tables (`paper_results.json`).

All models are Hugging Face exports (`Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`, bfloat16) of Qwen3.5 fine-tunes trained with
the recipes in `recipes/qwen3.5/`. Every export omits the base model's 15 `mtp.*` (multi-token prediction) tensors.
`model_identity_sha256` is the identity hash used in the released evaluation records (`reproduce/`), which ties
each checkpoint to its scores.

## Not planned for release

The other rows of the paper's tables (single-teacher students, uniform pool, dynamic router, annealed injection,
SeqKD-SFT, parameter merges) and the control runs (seeds 43/44, fixed and frozen weights, observe-only) can be
retrained with `recipes/qwen3.5/`, and their scores are in `reproduce/`.
